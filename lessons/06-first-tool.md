## what it is

A connection that lets your AI act instead of just answer. Your calendar, your email, your files. Without one, it writes the email and you still go send it.

## why you'd want it

Because right now you're the copy-paste in the middle, every single time. A tool takes you out of the middle.

## steal this

Hit the + in the chat → connectors → turn one on. Start with your calendar. Then ask “what does my week look like?” and watch it answer with your real week, not a guess.

## try it now

Turn on your calendar, then ask “what's on my week?” If it comes back with your actual meetings, it can act now, and “move my thursday call to friday” is the next thing to try. If your app doesn't show connectors on your plan, ask it to draft the message you'd send about your week and copy it in yourself. Same skill. You're just still the hands, for now.
