## what it is

The brain inside ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. It read an enormous amount of writing and got very good at guessing the next word.

## why you'd want it

Because once you know it's guessing from patterns, everything else it does makes sense, including when it's wrong.

## steal this

It's right a lot, and it has no built-in way to tell when it isn't. That's why you double-check.

## try it now

Open your chatbot and ask: “finish this sentence three different ways: the best thing about tuesdays is”. You'll get three perfectly fine answers and no way to tell which one is “true”. That's the whole lesson on one screen. It's picking likely words, not looking anything up.
